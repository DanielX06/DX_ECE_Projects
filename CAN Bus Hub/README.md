# U of A FSAE CAN Bus Hub
09/2025 - 10/2025

Goal: 
This project was to built for my FSAE team with two main goals in mind: reduces costs and gain engineering experience. Off the shelf hubs typically cost over $100 each. By designing and fabricating one myself, I can deliver a lower-cost solution tailored to our team's needs while also learning the design process.

Key Tasks:
-Design the schematic and pcb footprint for the CAN Hub
-Create a housing for the PCB

Learning Outcomes:
-Gained insight into implementing proper bus topology, and termination to ensure reliable communication across the subsystems
-Understanding transient voltage suppression (TVS) diodes to enhance circuit protection and safeguard components from voltage spikes
